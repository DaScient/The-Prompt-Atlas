## What students do — *not* what they read about

| Week | Quest |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rewrite a Fortune-500 shareholder letter as if it were owed to the CEO's great-great-granddaughter. |
| 2 | Audit a real watershed as if it were a shareholder. |
| **3** | **Co-write an origin myth with an AI — for the algorithm, the timezone, the password.** |
| 4 | Translate a 200-word passage across culture, century, and medium. |
| 5 | Brief a head of state on an open problem in physics, with footnotes on what the AI got wrong. |
| 6 | Design a symbiont for a species in crisis — and pre-mortem the harm. |
| 7 | Hold a seven-day mirror practice with an AI. Reply to what it saw. *(Midterm)* |
| 8 | Moot court: when does a machine deserve moral consideration? |
| 9 | Draft one article of the Martian Charter. Survive cross-examination. |
| 10 | Compress 5,000 words to six. In four stages. |
| 11 | Tabletop a crisis with the AI as oracle. Produce 48-hour and 10-year plans. |
| **12** | **Design an artifact intended to remain legible 10,000 years from now.** *(Final)* |
| 13 | **Festival of Futures** — public defense. Bring families, boards, the press. |

## Three things curriculum directors have told us they like

1. **"It comes with the artifact."** Twelve graded deliverables already specified. Rubrics already written. You aren't designing a course; you're calibrating one.
2. **"The transparency rubric makes AI cheating self-cancelling."** Students *must* annotate the human/AI split on every submission. An undeclared ghostwrite — even a brilliant one — caps at 2/4.
3. **"The Festival is a board-meeting magnet."** End-of-term public defenses. Parents come. Reporters come. School boards approve next year's section before the lights come up.

## How to adopt

| Option | Effort | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot one quest** | 1 week | Drop Week 3 (Co-Write a Myth) into any English or Honors section. |
| **Pilot one Part** | 2 weeks | Pick Part I (Weeks 1–2), Part IV (7–8), or Part VI (11–12). |
| **Run the full course** | 1 semester | Adopt the syllabus as a stand-alone elective or honors seminar. |
| **District-wide cohort** | 1 academic year | Train a cohort of teachers across schools using the shared Atlas Wall. We will co-design. |
